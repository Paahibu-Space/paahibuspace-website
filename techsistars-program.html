Aspiring Women in Tech

TechsiStars Mentorship Bootcamp

Bridging the gap in tech. This hybrid mentorship program connects aspiring girls with established female tech experts, offering mentorship and capacity-building opportunities to foster the next generation of leaders.

Why We Need This

TechsiStars addresses a critical gap. Women remain significantly underrepresented in technology leadership roles across Africa. We aim to dismantle barriers and build a pipeline of confident female tech leaders.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time commitment? expand_more
