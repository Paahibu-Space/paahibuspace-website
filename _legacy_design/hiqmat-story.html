The Spark

Bridging the Gender Digital Divide

Hiqmat's journey began with a simple observation: the digital divide wasn't just about access to devices, but about the safety and confidence to use them. Growing up in a community where tech was seen as a "boy's club," she saw how online harassment and lack of role models discouraged girls from pursuing tech careers.

Instead of accepting the status quo, she decided to become the change she wanted to see. She joined Paahibu Space, where she not only learned coding but also discovered her voice as an advocate.
