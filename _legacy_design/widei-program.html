Success Story

Meet Amina: From Market Stall to Online Enterprise

Amina, a 34-year-old fabric weaver, used to rely solely on foot traffic in her local market. Seasonal rains often meant zero income for weeks.

Through the WiDEI program, she digitized her inventory. We helped her set up a WhatsApp Business profile and taught her how to photograph her intricate patterns. Today, Amina receives orders from across the country, coordinates delivery via mobile money, and has hired two apprentices.
